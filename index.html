Non-Invasive Game Color Contrast Enhancement with Natural Color Preservation for Dichromats

UFRGS · Co-author · Best Paper, SIBGRAPI 2026

A real-time filter that raises color contrast for color-blind players without making the game look wrong: skin, grass and sky keep colors that still read as natural. It hooks into DirectX, Vulkan and OpenGL games without touching their source. We tested it on 18 commercial games and 2 mini-games built for the study, and players performed considerably better with it on.
